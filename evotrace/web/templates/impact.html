Two-Tier Impact Methodology

1. Static Dependency Intersection: Extracts referenced paths from registered declarative MongoDB query filters and compares them against proposed evolution paths:

  • High Risk Direct field destruction or renaming (e.g. postal_code).
  • Medium Risk Ancestor container or child sub-path restructuring.
  • Likely Unaffected Independent, disjoint field paths.

2. Empirical Query Verification: Executes the query filter against the current collection state to measure matching document volume, execution latency, and behavioral shifts.
